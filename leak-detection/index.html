How professional leak detection works

We confirm there's a leak

We start by checking your water meter and isolating fixtures and lines to confirm a leak exists and narrow down which system it's on: hot, cold, supply or drain.

We pinpoint the exact location

Using acoustic listening equipment, thermal imaging and line pressure testing, our technicians trace the leak to its precise location under the slab, inside a wall or underground, without guesswork.

We repair it the right way

You get your options (spot repair, reroute or repipe) with a flat, upfront price for each. We recommend what actually makes sense for your home, not the most expensive option.

Signs of a hidden leak

  • Sound of running water
  • Water meter moving with taps off
  • Damp carpet or warped flooring
  • Cracks in walls or flooring
  • Musty smell or mold
  • Low water pressure

Questions

Plumbing FAQ

How do I know if I have a slab leak?

Common signs are warm spots on the floor, an unexplained high water bill, the sound of running water when everything is off, cracks in flooring, or low water pressure. We confirm it with electronic detection before any work.

Will you have to tear up my floors or walls?

Our goal is to open as little as possible. Pinpoint leak detection lets us access only the exact spot of the leak, or reroute the line entirely to avoid breaking the slab.

What causes pipe leaks?

Corrosion in older copper pipes, high water pressure, hard water, shifting soil and failed fittings are the most common causes. Recurring leaks are often a sign it's time to consider a repipe.

What should I do if a pipe bursts?

Shut off your main water valve right away, turn off power near any standing water and call our 24/7 line at 800-930-2476.

Does homeowners insurance cover slab leaks?

Many policies cover resulting water damage and access costs, though coverage varies. We provide documentation of our findings to help with your claim.

Think you have a leak?

The longer a leak runs, the more damage it does. Schedule leak detection today, or call 24/7 for burst pipes.
