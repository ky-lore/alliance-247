What does generator installation cost?

What affects generator installation cost

Cost depends mainly on generator size (kW), whether you want whole-home or essential-circuit backup, fuel type (natural gas or propane), the transfer switch, distance from the panel and gas meter, concrete pad requirements, and permits.

Questions

Electrical FAQ

How much does a whole home generator installation cost?

Generator installation cost depends on the size (kW), fuel type, transfer switch, distance to the panel and gas meter, pad and permit requirements. We provide a free, written, flat-price estimate after a quick site visit.

What size generator do I need for my home?

It depends on what you want to power. Running central A/C and the whole house needs a larger unit than keeping essentials on. We do a load assessment to size it correctly.

How does a home standby generator work?

It's permanently installed outside, runs on natural gas or propane, and connects through an automatic transfer switch. When the power goes out, it starts and takes over automatically.

Do I need a permit to install a generator?

Yes. Standby generators typically require electrical and, for gas units, mechanical/plumbing permits. We handle the permits and inspections.

Do you service generators?

Yes. We offer generator maintenance, repairs and load testing so your system is ready when an outage hits.
