What to do in a plumbing emergency

1. Shut off the water

Find your main water shut-off valve (often near the front hose bib or where the line enters the house) and turn it off. For a single fixture, use the valve under the sink or behind the toilet.

2. Protect people and property

Turn off power to any area where water is near outlets or appliances. Move valuables out of the way and start removing standing water if it's safe to do so. If you smell gas, leave the home and call 911 or your gas company first.

3. Call a 24/7 emergency plumber

Call The Alliance 247 at 800-930-2476. Our emergency plumbers are on call 24 hours a day, 7 days a week across Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside and San Bernardino counties, and you'll get an upfront price before we start any repair.
