Questions

Heating FAQ

How often should I service my furnace?

Once a year — ideally in the fall before you rely on it. A tune-up keeps it efficient, safe and less likely to fail on the coldest night.

My furnace runs but the house is cold — what's wrong?

It could be anything from a clogged filter or bad thermostat to a failing blower or heat exchanger. We'll diagnose it precisely rather than guessing.

Is a heat pump worth it in Southern California?

Often yes — our mild climate is ideal for heat pumps, which heat and cool efficiently in one system. We'll help you compare options.
