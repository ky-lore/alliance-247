Questions

Electrical FAQ

Do I need a panel upgrade?

If you have an older or overloaded panel, frequently tripped breakers, or you're adding major appliances or an EV charger, an upgrade is often needed. We'll assess your load and advise honestly.

Can you install an EV charger in my garage?

Yes — we install Level 2 home chargers, including any needed circuit and panel work, cleanly and to code.

Why do my lights flicker?

Flickering can be a loose connection, an overloaded circuit or a failing panel — some causes are safety hazards. It's worth having it checked promptly, and we can diagnose it fast.
