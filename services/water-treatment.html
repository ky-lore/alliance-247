Questions

Water treatment FAQ

Is Southern California water really that hard?

Yes — much of the region has very hard water, which leaves scale on fixtures and inside appliances. A softener dramatically reduces buildup and spotting.

What's the difference between a softener and a filter?

A softener removes the minerals that cause hardness and scale. A filter removes things like chlorine, sediment and taste/odor contaminants. Many homes benefit from both.

Do you offer salt-free options?

Yes. Salt-free conditioners reduce scale without adding sodium and require less maintenance — we'll help you decide what fits your needs.
