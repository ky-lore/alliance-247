Questions

Air conditioning FAQ

How often should I service my AC?

Once a year, ideally in spring before the heat hits. A tune-up improves efficiency and helps prevent mid-summer breakdowns.

My AC is blowing warm air — is it the refrigerant?

Possibly, but it could also be a dirty coil, failing capacitor or frozen line. Low refrigerant usually means a leak, which we'll locate and repair rather than just refill.

How long does a new AC install take?

Most residential replacements are completed in a single day. We'll give you a clear timeline with your estimate.
