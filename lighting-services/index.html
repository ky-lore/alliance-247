Choosing an outdoor lighting company

Why use a licensed electrician

Many outdoor lighting companies are installers, not electricians. When new circuits, outdoor-rated boxes, GFCI protection or line-voltage fixtures are involved, you want a licensed electrician who does it to code and pulls permits where needed.

Outdoor lighting installation

We design and install landscape, path, patio, security and architectural lighting (low-voltage or line-voltage) with timers, photocells or smart controls so it just works.

Outdoor lighting repair

Water intrusion, corroded connections, failed transformers and damaged wiring are the most common causes of outdoor lighting failure. We find the fault and fix it. Licensed, bonded and insured (CSLB #1119845).

Questions

Electrical FAQ

Do I need an electrician to install a light fixture?

Replacing a fixture involves working with live household wiring, so a licensed electrician is the safest choice, especially when a new box, switch or circuit is needed or the fixture is heavy, like a chandelier or ceiling fan.

Do you install outdoor and landscape lighting?

Yes. We install landscape, path, patio, security and architectural lighting, low-voltage and line-voltage, with timers, photocells or smart controls.

Why did my outdoor lights stop working?

Common causes are a tripped GFCI, a failed transformer or timer, water in a fixture, corroded connections or damaged underground wire. We'll diagnose and repair it.

How much does lighting installation cost?

It depends on the number and type of fixtures and whether new wiring is needed. We provide free estimates on new installations with a flat, upfront price.

What areas do you serve?

Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside and San Bernardino counties, with coverage available from Ventura to San Diego County.
