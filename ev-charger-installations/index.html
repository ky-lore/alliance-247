What goes into a home EV charger installation

Level 2 EV charger installation

A Level 2 charger runs on 240 volts and can add a full night's worth of range while you sleep, far faster than a standard 120V outlet. We install hardwired units and 240V outlets for plug-in chargers.

Tesla Wall Connector installation

We install and configure Tesla Wall Connectors for your home's available electrical capacity, including multiple connectors with power sharing when you have more than one EV.

Will I need a panel upgrade?

Not always. We do a load calculation first. Many homes can add an EV charger without an upgrade; older or smaller services may need load management or a panel upgrade. Either way, you'll get one flat, upfront price. Licensed, bonded and insured (CSLB #1119845).

Questions

Electrical FAQ

How much does EV charger installation cost?

It depends on the distance from your panel to where you park, your panel's available capacity, the charger type and permit requirements. We give you a free, written, flat-price estimate after a quick assessment.

Do you install the Tesla Wall Connector?

Yes. We install and configure Tesla Wall Connectors, including power-sharing setups for homes with more than one Tesla.

What's the difference between Level 1 and Level 2 charging?

Level 1 uses a standard 120V outlet and adds range slowly. Level 2 uses a 240V circuit and charges several times faster, enough to fully charge most EVs overnight.

Will I need to upgrade my electrical panel?

Not always. We'll do a load calculation first. Some homes can add a charger as-is; others need load management or a panel upgrade, which we can also handle.

Do you pull permits for EV charger installation?

Yes. We pull the required permit and schedule the inspection.
