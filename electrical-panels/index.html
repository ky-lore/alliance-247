What does an electrical panel upgrade cost?

What affects panel cost

Every home is different. Panel upgrade cost depends on the amperage you need (for example 100A to 200A), whether the meter or service entrance needs to be replaced, utility coordination, permit and inspection requirements, and the condition of existing wiring.

Free, written estimates

Instead of a ballpark over the phone, we inspect your current panel and service, talk through what you plan to add (EV charger, solar, A/C, a remodel) and give you a free, written, flat-price estimate. The price you approve is the price you pay.

Questions

Electrical FAQ

How much does it cost to upgrade an electrical panel?

Panel cost depends on the amperage, whether the meter and service entrance need replacing, permit fees and the condition of your wiring. We give you a free, written, flat-price estimate after a quick on-site inspection.

Do I need a 200 amp panel?

If you're adding an EV charger, solar, a heat pump, a spa or a major remodel, a 200A service is often recommended. We'll do a load assessment and tell you honestly whether you need it.

How long does a panel upgrade take?

Most residential panel swaps are completed in a day. Power is off for part of that time while the utility disconnects and reconnects service.

Do you pull permits for panel upgrades?

Yes. We pull the required permits, schedule the inspection and coordinate with your utility.

Can you just repair my panel instead of replacing it?

Often, yes. If the panel is in good condition we'll repair it by replacing a breaker or fixing a connection. We only recommend an upgrade when it's needed.
